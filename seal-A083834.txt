# SEAL-A083834 - seal of the A083834 theorems

Date: 2026-11-15. Produced on an external Linux runner
(Lean 4.35.0 toolchain, pinned below).

## Sealed objects

Two kernel-checked declarations, exported independently:

1. `proven_A083834 : SpecA083834` (Check_Spec_A083834.lean),
   the conjunction of the two public theorems:
   - `A083834_C1` (= `A083834_alphabet`): every digit of
     q = 5p+1 lies in {0,1,5,6};
   - `A083834_C2` (= `A083834_repunit`): m' even =>
     q = repunit.
2. `A083834_starts6_odd` (A083834.lean): if the leading
   digit dgt(5p+1)(m'-1) of q equals 6, then m' is odd.
   This is the literal-reading corollary of C2 (an even-length
   output would be all 1's, and 6 != 1).

The serialized transitive closures cover the carry-chain
development (module A083833.lean + spec + wrapper).

## Limits

- The seal covers the two declarations above; the two public
  theorems A083834_alphabet / A083834_repunit are direct
  projections / wrapper of the first.
- Single production run; tool hashes pin the exact binaries
  (toolchain v4.35.0).
- The statement A083834_starts6_odd assumes m' >= 1
  (nonempty output), matching the intended domain
  p >= 1 (0 is not a member of A083833).
